# 01 — Problem & Vision

## The Problem Statement (SIH26128)

**Organization:** Government of Maharashtra (Dept. of Animal Husbandry & Dairying)
**Title:** Efficient systems for early detection, prevention, and management of livestock diseases and animal health issues
**Category:** Software · Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
**Deadline:** 20 September 2026

---

## The Real-World Problem (in plain terms)

India has **535.78 million registered livestock animals.** When a disease like Foot & Mouth, Lumpy Skin, or PPR breaks out:

1. **Farmers report late** — they don't recognize early symptoms, or don't know who to call
2. **Diagnosis is far away** — the nearest vet may be 20-50 km away
3. **Records are fragmented** — farm records, dispensary records, lab records, and government surveillance data all live in separate silos
4. **No early warning** — by the time officials realize an outbreak is happening, it has already spread across villages
5. **Zoonotic risk** — diseases like Anthrax and Brucellosis spread to humans
6. **Economic devastation** — FMD alone costs India ₹20,000+ crore annually in lost milk/meat production

The core gap: **there is no unified, real-time system that connects the farmer at the village level to the government at the state level.**

---

## Our Vision

> **PashuRaksha turns every farmer's smartphone into a disease-surveillance sensor, and every government office into a real-time command center.**

Not a vet-consultation app. Not a record-keeping app. A **public-health early-warning system** — the livestock equivalent of a weather warning network.

### The one-line pitch
"We detect the outbreak before it becomes an epidemic — using AI at the farm, geospatial intelligence at the district, and automated alerts across the entire chain."

---

## Who Uses It (6 Stakeholders)

| Role | What they do | What PashuRaksha gives them |
|------|-------------|----------------------------|
| **Farmer** | Owns animals, first to see symptoms | AI diagnosis by photo/voice, treatment advice, area outbreak warnings |
| **Field Officer** | Ground-level surveillance | Report cases, field inspection logging, cluster alerts |
| **Veterinarian** | Treats animals, confirms diagnosis | Case queue, lab dispatch, treatment protocols |
| **Lab Technician** | Tests samples | Sample tracking pipeline, result upload |
| **Govt Official** | District/state oversight | Live outbreak map, epidemiological curves, vaccination gaps |
| **Admin** | System management | User management, data sync, infrastructure |

---

## Why This Wins vs Existing Apps (DrPashu, etc.)

| Private vet apps | PashuRaksha |
|------------------|-------------|
| Connect 1 farmer to 1 vet (paid) | Free public-health infrastructure for all |
| No outbreak awareness | Spatial clustering detects outbreaks automatically |
| No government integration | Full 6-role pipeline: farmer → govt |
| Individual problem | System-level epidemic prevention |
| English only | 8 Indian languages, voice-first for uneducated farmers |

**The distinction:** Private apps solve "my animal is sick." PashuRaksha solves "how do we stop 5 lakh animals from getting sick."
