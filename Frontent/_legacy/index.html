Operations Workflow

Detect → Contain → Vaccinate → Respond

The four-step cycle keeps every district ahead of the next outbreak.

01

Report

Farmers & officers file case / symptom reports from any device, online or offline.

02

Analyze

Lab and AI triage pins the pathogen and flags containment risk instantly.

03

Dispatch

Nearest RRT / MVU auto-assigned and directed to the exact GPS coordinate.

04

Resolve

Vaccination, quarantine and follow-up close the case on record — fully audit-able.
